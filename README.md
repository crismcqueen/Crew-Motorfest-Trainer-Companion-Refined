## ✨ Feature Highlights

Every feature below was crafted with intent. Here's what lives under the hood:

### 🎯 Responsive UI That Breathes
The interface adapts to your window size, your screen resolution, and even your input method. Resize it mid-session and the layout reflows instantly — no scrambled panels, no clipped labels. It feels alive, like a dashboard that understands the road.

### 🌍 Multilingual Support (12+ Languages)
Racing is universal, and so is this Companion. Ship with support for English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Polish, and Arabic. Language switching happens on the fly with zero restart required.

### 🕛 24/7 Customer Support
An engine doesn't care what time zone you're in, and neither do we. Round-the-clock assistance means questions get answered while you're still mid-lap. Weekend, holiday, 3 AM — the pit crew is awake.

### 🧩 Modular Toggle System
Each capability sits in its own capsule. Enable what you need, silence what you don't. The modular design means toggling one option never accidentally drags another along for the ride.

### 🖥️ Native Windows Optimization
Built to coexist peacefully with Windows 11 and Windows 10, including modern GPU scheduling, high-DPI scaling, and Game Mode awareness. No stutter tax, no background bloat.

### 💾 Lightweight Footprint
The Companion sips resources rather than gulping them. Your framerate stays where it belongs: on the track.

### 🔄 Seamless Season Sync
When the game evolves, the Companion follows. Configuration profiles persist across releases so your preferences never evaporate.

### 🎨 Custom Profile Manager
Save multiple personas — one for casual cruising, one for hardcore tuning, one for exploration sessions. Swap between them in a single click.

### 🔐 Local-Only Configuration
Every setting stays on your machine. The Companion respects privacy as much as it respects performance.

### 📊 Real-Time Diagnostics Panel
A small, elegant readout of what the Companion is doing, so nothing feels like a mystery box.

The Companion learns the rhythm of your sessions, remembers your last profile, and quietly waits until you're ready. It's the difference between a wrench and a trusted mechanic.

## 🧑‍🔧 User Experience Philosophy

We believe the best software disappears. When you sit down to play, you shouldn't be reading a manual — you should be shifting gears. Every screen, every menu, every label in the Companion was pressure-tested to feel like an extension of the game rather than an interruption of it.

Three principles guide every commit:

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** If a label has to be explained, it's the wrong label.
2. **Speed over spectacle.** Animations are subtle and never delay input.
3. **Respect over reach.** Your data, your machine, your rules.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Companion safe to use alongside other utilities?**
Yes. It plays well with overlay tools, capture software, and performance monitors. Conflict-free by design.

**Do I need to be technically savvy to use it?**
Not at all. If you can click a button, you can use the Companion. Power users get extra depth, but beginners get a smooth start.

**Will my settings survive a game update?**
Yes — profiles are stored independently and persist across game patches.

**How often is the Companion updated?**
Major updates typically roll out monthly, with hotfixes arriving as needed during seasonal content drops.

**Can I use it on multiple PCs?**
Absolutely. Profiles are portable and can be moved manually between machines.
